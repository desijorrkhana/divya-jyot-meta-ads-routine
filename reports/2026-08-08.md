columns out to **col AF**. Any lead dialed more than 4 times, or any post-visit lead with more than a
few follow-up notes, had its **most recent** contact history invisible to every report using the old
range — the truncation always cuts off the newest entries, so it systematically made recently-active

untouched on 7 Aug (they weren't — see FOLLOW-UP above), and Jagdish Ravasia {Sushma} looked 8-9 days
